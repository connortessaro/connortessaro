### What I'm building

**[Kizuki](https://kizuki.dev)** is a study tool for your terminal. You explain a concept from memory, and Kizuki asks about what you got wrong, quoting your course material word for word. [Code](https://github.com/connortessaro/kizuki) · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/kizuki)

**[Ringi](https://ringi.dev)** is a Slack app for team decisions. It asks each person for their view in private, finds where they disagree, and posts one brief with a recommendation.

**[Phantom AI](https://phantom.codes)** is an AI API that works with OpenAI's client libraries. You pay for a key up front, and each call returns a signed receipt with the model, the tokens and the cost. Agents use its command-line tool, `phantom`, to make keys with their own budgets and to buy credit from a wallet. [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@phantomcodes/cli)
